Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026.

Facteur Commun Stats is an internal tool used only by the owner of the Facteur Commun channel. It reads the statistics of the channel's own videos (views, likes, comments, shares, follower count) from YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, after the channel owner has authorized access.

Data collected

Only aggregated statistics of the channel's own account and videos. No data about other users is collected.

Use

The data is used only to produce internal performance reports for the channel. It is never sold, shared or used for advertising.

Storage and deletion

Access tokens are stored on a private server, in files readable only by the owner. Access can be revoked at any time from the platform's account settings, after which no further data is read.
